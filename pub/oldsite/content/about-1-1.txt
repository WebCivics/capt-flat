1938 is the Captains Flat Hotel's intimate whisky bar—a refined space that pays tribute to the year the hotel's iconic Art Deco building first opened. Tucked away from the main bar, 1938 blends heritage charm with modern comfort, offering a relaxed atmosphere where guests can slow down and savour a quality dram.

Featuring a carefully curated selection of Australian and international whiskies, premium spirits, local wines and classic cocktails, it's the perfect place to unwind  or settle in for a quiet evening with friends. Rich timber, warm lighting and timeless Art Deco styling create an inviting space that honours the hotel's remarkable history while offering a fresh experience for locals and visitors alike.

Whether you're a seasoned whisky enthusiast or simply looking for somewhere special to enjoy an evening, 1938 is where history, hospitality and exceptional drinks come together in the heart of Captains Flat. The bar takes its name from the year the hotel was built—a landmark Art Deco building that was once renowned for having one of Australia's longest bars and remains one of the town's defining heritage landmarks.
