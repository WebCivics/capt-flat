Welcome to The Flat

A proper country pub,
like they used to make them

Personal service and hospitality in a unique heritage country pub. Our landmark Art Deco building was once renowned for having one of Australia's longest bars — and it remains one of the town's defining landmarks.

Come for a cold draught by the wood fire, stay for a parmi, and try your luck at floating a schooner glass on our famous wall. Stick it, and you've earned Bloody Legend status.

What we offer

Three good reasons to pull in

A Welcoming Bar

Relax with a cold draught beer in front of the wood fireplace, or slip into 1938 — our intimate whisky bar.

Cosy Rooms

Stay overnight in one of our basic but comfortable heritage rooms — cast iron beds and all.

Quality Pub Meals

An extensive menu of pub classics, including vegetarian options and a kids menu for the little legends.

The Whisky Bar

Step into 1938

Tucked away from the main bar, 1938 blends heritage charm with modern comfort — rich timber, warm lighting and timeless Art Deco styling.

A carefully curated selection of Australian and international whiskies, premium spirits, local wines and classic cocktails.
