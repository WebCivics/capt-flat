Our Rooms

Simple, comfortable, full of character

Single Room

Features an original 1937 cast iron single bed, solid oak Art Deco wardrobe, cosy woollen blanket and an electric blanket for those chilly nights.

$90 per night

Twin Single

Two original 1937 cast iron single beds — don’t worry, the mattresses have been replaced since then! Solid oak custom-made wardrobe & electric blankets.

$110 per night

Double & Queen Rooms

One double or queen bed — some rooms with balcony access over the main street.

$110 per night

Accommodation is available 7 days a week, with the option of a continental breakfast. Please note the pub is closed on Monday & Tuesday, so there is no food or drink service on those days. To book, call (02) 6236 6069 or say g’day at captainsflathub@gmail.com. Travelling with a caravan or camper? Park the rig and take a room for the night — or see the donation camping at Wilkins Park.
